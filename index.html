Stranger things

Stranger Things is a sci-fi horror series set in 1980s Hawkins, Indiana, where a group of kids, a worried mother, and a police chief uncover supernatural mysteries and secret government experiments after a young boy vanishes, leading them to a terrifying alternate dimension called the Upside Down and a psychokinetic girl named Eleven.
